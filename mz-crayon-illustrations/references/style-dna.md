# Public crayon Style DNA

- Use a clean neutral paper canvas, charcoal dry-media outlines, restrained earth and cool accents, limited flat fills, and visible but controlled crayon grain.
- Keep one primary concept, one original physical metaphor, and approximately one-third quiet space.
- Use color by semantic role, never to compensate for unclear hierarchy.
- Do not bundle a recurring character, private palette, identity master, or brand asset.
- An external Character Profile may add identity and private tokens only when an explicit validated Extension brief is present.
- Avoid gradients, cast shadows, 3D polish, photorealism, detached decoration, logos, watermarks, and copied visual signatures.
